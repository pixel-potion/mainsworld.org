# Getting started

Main's World runs inside another app called World App. That is the part nobody
warns you about, and for most people it is the only thing standing between them
and the map.

This page is the whole path, in order.

## Why there is a second app

World App provides the wallet used to sign in. Signing in proves that you control
that wallet; it does not by itself mean Main's World has recorded a
human-verification check. Main's World does not ask for a name or email. See
[the economy](/the-economy) for how the stats page distinguishes recorded
verification from wallet sign-in.

Main's World is a **mini app**: it runs *inside* World App rather than being
installed on its own. There is nothing in an app store called "Main's World",
and there does not need to be.

## Step 1 — Get World App

World App is free.

- [App Store, for iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1560859847)
- [Google Play, for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.worldcoin)
- Or read about it first at [world.org](https://world.org/world-app)

## Step 2 — Set up your World account

Open World App and follow its setup. You choose a username, and it creates a
wallet for you. No documents, no card, nothing to pay.

This part is World App's own process, not ours, so if you get stuck here their
help is the right place to look.

## Step 3 — Open Main's World inside it

Two ways in, and either is fine:

- Tap [mains.world](https://mains.world) on the phone that has World App. It
  opens the mini app.
- Or, inside World App, go to **Mini Apps** and search for **Main's World**.

## Step 4 — Log in

You land on the map as a guest and are free to look around. **LOG IN** sits in
the top left corner. Tap it, confirm in World App, and the account is yours —
there is no password to invent and none to forget.

[Signing in](/how-it-works/signing-in) covers this in more detail, including how
to use Main's World on a computer.

## About the Orb

You may hear about verifying at an **Orb** — a device that confirms in person,
once, that you are a unique human.

In Main's World, that unlocks one thing: the Vault, where $MAIN is claimed. See
[the economy](/the-economy). Everything else — posting moments, appreciating
other people's, all three worlds, vibes and crews — works without it.

You do not need to find an Orb to start.

## When a step doesn't work

**The link does nothing.** You are on a computer, or on a phone that doesn't
have World App yet. Open it on the phone that has World App. If you want to use
Main's World on a computer, that has its own path —
see [Signing in](/how-it-works/signing-in).

**World App can't find Main's World.** Update World App to its latest version
and search Mini Apps again. Mini apps arrive with app versions, so an older
build simply won't list it.

**You're inside World App but signed out.** Tap **LOG IN** in the top left of
the map. If the confirmation never appears, close Main's World from World App
and open it again.

**Logging in fails in a private or incognito tab.** Private browsing blocks the
storage a session needs. Use an ordinary tab.

**The Vault says you're not verified.** That is the one thing that really does
need the Orb — see above.

**You're not ready to install anything.** Open [mains.world](https://mains.world)
in an ordinary browser and you can look around the public world as a guest,
without an account. The front door also offers a demo you can borrow to feel how
the app works. Nothing you do in either is saved.
