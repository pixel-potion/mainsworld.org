## Status legend

- **Connected**: a bespoke partner integration exists today.
- **First party**: a Main's World capability, not a submitted partner app.
- **Coming soon**: represented in SPACE but not connected or callable.
- **Proposed**: a reviewed public listing that is not connected or activated.
- **Wishlist**: a future possibility with no connector promised.
