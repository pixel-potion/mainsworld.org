# Roadmap

Here's where Main's World is going. These are **directions, not dated promises** —
plans evolve as we learn and as the community weighs in. We keep this page honest
about what's live versus what's still ahead.

## Vibes, in full

Live vibes work today, and so does planning one ahead — with a start, an end,
and a place, and RSVPs before it begins (see [Vibes](/how-it-works/vibes)).
What's still ahead:

- **Invite anyone.** Bring in friends, and even hold a spot for someone who
  hasn't joined Main's World yet.

## A clearer way around

The app's navigation is being reshaped around the two things that matter most:
**vibes** (live gatherings) and **moments** (the photos and videos people
share), with a
simple way to create and to search.

## Following

Alongside mutual connections, a lighter, one-way **follow** — for keeping up with
someone you met at a vibe, or a creator you admire, without asking them to
connect back. Following will stay public-world only; it never crosses into
anyone's private space.

## Crews

Turning the groups that form around vibes into lasting **[crews](/how-it-works/crews)**
that can create together and share what they earn — with fair, member-approved
rules.

## SPACE — a future world

Today's worlds (SKY, LAND, DEEP) are about *where you physically are*. **SPACE**
is an idea for a fourth world that's about *where you are among people* — a home
for gatherings that aren't tied to a physical spot, so you can be together with
people far away. It's an early concept, shared here so it can grow in the open.
