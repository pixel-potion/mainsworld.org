# Whitepaper

:::note Coming soon
The Whitepaper is being written. This page will hold the full, reasoned argument
for Main's World and for SHIP. Here's the outline it will follow.
:::

The Whitepaper makes the complete case, for readers who want the depth behind the
[Manifesto](/manifesto) and [What is SHIP](/what-is-ship). Planned sections:

1. **The problem** — how the attention economy works, and why bots and
   machine-generated content are breaking trust online.
2. **The insight** — what changes when every account is provably a real human,
   and how that's possible without sacrificing privacy.
3. **The model** — the worlds, tapes, vibes, and crews; and the appreciation
   economy that ties them together.
4. **Why it's durable** — why this is hard for attention-first platforms to copy.
5. **Roadmap** — where Main's World is headed.
6. **Open questions** — what we're still figuring out, in the open.

*Want to help? Like everything here, the Whitepaper is community-editable — see
[Contribute](/contribute).*
