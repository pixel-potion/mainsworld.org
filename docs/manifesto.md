# Manifesto

Something has quietly gone wrong with being online.

We were promised connection. We got feeds built to keep us scrolling. We were
promised a global village, and got a place where you can't always tell who's a
person and who's a machine wearing a person's face. The tools meant to bring us
together started measuring us instead — counting our attention, selling it, and
calling that community.

It doesn't have to be this way. So we're building something else.

## What we believe

**Everyone online should be a real person.** Not a bot, not a farm, not a
stranger's second face. Just people.

**You can be real and still be private.** You shouldn't have to hand over your
name, your face, or your data to have a seat at the table. Being anonymous should
never mean being surrounded by fakes.

**Connecting is the point** — and the kindest thing an app can do is help you
connect, then let you go live your life. Not keep you.

**A moment matters most when it's true:** a real person, in a real place, at a
real time. Not content to be optimized. A memory to be kept.

**The only thing worth counting is genuine appreciation between people** — and it
should only ever be given, never taken, and never faked.

**Attention is not care. Reach is not friendship. A number is not a life.**

## The world we're building

Main's World is our answer: a map for moments made by people, with privacy built
in. We are working toward stronger, privacy-preserving human verification; today
wallet sign-in proves wallet control, and verification is recorded separately. A
place you visit to find your people — across time, across distance — not a place
designed to hold on to you.

We call this kind of place a **SHIP: a Secure Human Interaction Protocol.** It's a
small idea with a large consequence. If people can be recognized as human without
giving up privacy, everything built on top of that can be warmer, calmer, and more
honest.

None of this is nostalgia for an internet that never existed. It's a wager that
the next one can be better — quieter where it should be quiet, generous where it
should be generous, and human all the way down.

## Come as you are

The internet can be good for people again. We think that's worth building, and
worth building in the open — which is why even these words are yours to improve.

If any of this sounds like the world you want to be part of, there's a place for
you here. You're a Main. Welcome.
