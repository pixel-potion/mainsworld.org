# Manifesto

:::note Coming soon
The full Manifesto is being written. What's below is the essence — the finished
version will land here shortly.
:::

We believe the internet can be good for people again.

We believe you should be able to be yourself online without handing over your
name, your email, or your data — and that being anonymous should never mean
being surrounded by bots.

We believe the point of connecting is to connect, and then to go live your
life — not to be kept scrolling.

We believe a moment matters most when it's real: a real person, in a real place,
at a real time.

We believe the only thing worth measuring is genuine appreciation between real
people — and that you should be able to give it freely.

We're building a place that runs on all of that. It's called Main's World.
