# Privacy Policy

**Effective and last updated: August 24, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Pixel Potion Creative LLC** ("Pixel Potion,"
"we," "us," or "our"), based in Austin, Texas, USA, handles personal
information for Main's World on **mains.world**, **mainsworld.org**, and Main's
World within **World App** (together, the **Service**). Contact us at
[hello@mains.world](mailto:hello@mains.world).

This Policy covers information Pixel Potion handles for the Service. World App,
World ID, World Chain, and related World services are provided by other
organizations and have their own terms and privacy notices. Review the current
[World Privacy Notice](https://world.org/legal/privacy-notice) and the provider
notices identified in the [World ID legal
center](https://world.org/legal/world-id-user-terms-and-privacy).

## 1. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

- wallet and authentication identifiers, World verification status, profile
  information, usernames, and connection choices;
- moments, media, comments, reactions, captions, and related metadata;
- precise or coarse location and location history when a feature requires it;
- device, notification, session, browser, and operational information;
- payment, credit, $MAIN, and transaction records where those features are
  used;
- support messages and the information you include in them; and
- security, moderation, and operational logs.

We may receive information from World or World App when you choose to connect
or use the Service through them, and from providers that support
authentication, hosting, payments, security, moderation, and operations.

## 2. How we use information

We use information to provide and improve the Service; authenticate users;
maintain profiles, connections, and chosen sharing settings; show moments and
Vibes; provide support; process applicable transactions; protect people and the
Service; prevent fraud and abuse; comply with law; and communicate about service
matters.

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not share personal
information for cross-context behavioral advertising or use it for targeted
advertising.

Depending on the context and applicable law, we process information because it
is needed to provide the Service you request, because we have legitimate
interests in operating and protecting the Service, with your consent, or to
meet legal obligations. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw
that consent prospectively.

## 3. Sharing, audiences, and special links

We share information with providers that need it to operate the Service,
including Cloudflare for hosting and observability, Supabase for application
services, World and World App where relevant, Anthropic for AI-assisted safety,
and providers for authentication, payments, security, and support. Each
provider handles information under its own agreement and privacy terms.

Mapbox receives coordinates and search terms needed to provide maps, search,
and geocoding. The National Weather Service and other public alert providers
may receive rounded coordinates needed to return nearby safety alerts.

We may also disclose information when required or reasonably necessary to
comply with law, enforce our terms, protect rights and safety, investigate abuse
or fraud, or complete a corporate transaction such as a merger, financing, or
acquisition.

What you share depends on the audience you choose. SKY is public and can include
precise location. Unauthorized LAND viewers may see only a locked,
approximately 110-meter location hint without identity or content; authorized
LAND viewers receive the full view, including precise location. DEEP provides
no location hint to unauthorized viewers. Authorized Vibe viewers and valid
special-link holders receive the complete view allowed for that exact content.
Public content and profile information may be viewed, copied, or shared by
others.

A special link is a bearer secret for a complete read-only view of its exact
Moment or Vibe. Depending on the content, that view can include precise
location, comments, otherwise non-public Vibe moments, and a privacy-trimmed
RunPal route or GPX. A signed-out Vibe-link holder remains a read-only guest. An
authenticated holder of an exact Vibe link may automatically join that Vibe
after server validation. Except for that exact-link authenticated Vibe
auto-join, special links grant no other write access; posting, commenting,
reacting, and other actions require the applicable account and authorization.

Special links may last 1 hour, 1 day, 7 days, or 30 days (7 days by default),
and a Vibe link cannot last beyond 24 hours after the event ends. Links can be
revoked, expire, or be invalidated when the underlying content is deleted. Do
not share a special link publicly if its content is sensitive.

## 4. AI-assisted safety and privacy warnings

Main's World currently uses Anthropic's Claude for two distinct purposes:

1. **Mandatory SKY moderation.** SKY-bound posts, edits, and comments send the
   submitted text and supported images needed for automated safety review. The
   system may block public placement when it identifies sexual or explicit
   material, hate, graphic violence or gore, clearly illegal content, or cannot
   confidently clear the submission for a broad public-facing audience. The gate
   also fails closed if the review is unavailable. SKY also rejects
   provenance-declared or high-confidence AI-generated images, although these
   signals are imperfect and may miss generated images or flag genuine ones. A
   refusal is visible in the administrator queue for seven days. Signed access
   to a quarantine copy of refused SKY media expires after seven days. The
   underlying quarantine copy is removed by a later administrator cleanup, not
   necessarily at that seven-day boundary, and refusal records may remain in
   operational storage after leaving the queue. An administrator may override
   a false positive for a new post. There is currently no user-facing appeal
   feature.
2. **Advisory privacy assistant.** A transient, downscaled image can be sent to
   Claude to identify possible privacy concerns such as homes, children,
   bystanders, screens or documents, license plates, and identity or location
   exposure. Pixel Potion discards that assist image after the response. The
   assistant may show strong warnings, but it is advisory and does not block
   posting.

We do not use facial recognition or biometric profiling, and we do not train
Pixel Potion's own general-purpose AI models on user content. Anthropic
processes submitted material as our service provider under its applicable
terms.

## 5. Browser storage, analytics, and logs

We use essential browser storage, including session or local storage, for
authentication, settings, and guest-invite handoff. We may use Cloudflare's
cookie-free Web Analytics and operational logs to understand performance and
protect the Service. You can control some browser storage through your browser
settings, though disabling essential storage may affect how the Service works.

## 6. Retention and deletion

We keep active account and content information while needed to provide the
Service. You can edit or delete content through available features. To request
account deletion, email [hello@mains.world](mailto:hello@mains.world). We do not
maintain a separate archive of deleted content.

Refused SKY items leave the administrator queue after seven days, and signed
access to quarantine media expires after seven days. Underlying quarantine
copies are removed by a later administrator cleanup, and refusal records may
remain in operational storage after leaving the queue. Deletion cannot erase
public-blockchain records. Provider backups, fraud and security records, or
records required by law may take a limited time to age out or may remain where
technically or legally required.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect
information. No service or transmission is completely secure, so use care with
credentials, special-link secrets, and sensitive content.

## 8. International users and your choices

The Service may be used from outside the United States, and information may be
processed in the United States or other places where we or our providers
operate. Those locations may have different data-protection laws than your home
country.

Where applicable, you may request access to, correction of, export of, or
deletion of personal information by emailing
[hello@mains.world](mailto:hello@mains.world). We may need to verify your
identity and may limit a request where allowed or required by law. Depending on
where you live, you may also have rights to object to or restrict certain
processing, withdraw consent, appeal a denied privacy request, or complain to a
data-protection authority.

information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information,
contact [hello@mains.world](mailto:hello@mains.world).
